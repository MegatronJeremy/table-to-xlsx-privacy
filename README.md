# Privacy policy: Table to XLSX

Last updated: 2026-10-12

**Short version:** this extension does not collect your data. The free features never use the network.

1. **Table contents, page addresses and titles** stay in your browser. They are read only to build the file you ask for and are never sent anywhere.
2. **Free users:** nothing is stored and nothing is sent.
3. **Pro licence check:** if you buy Pro and enter a licence key, the extension sends the key and the product's identifier to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) to check that it is valid and has not been refunded or disputed. This happens when you click Activate, and then at most once every 7 days while Pro is active (if a check fails it is retried no more often than every 6 hours). No cookies are sent. Your IP address is visible to Gumroad as with any web request.
4. **What is stored on your computer:** the licence key and two timestamps, in the browser's extension storage. "Remove licence" on the extension's options page, or Gumroad reporting the key invalid, refunded or disputed, deletes them.
5. **Offline:** Pro stays active for up to 30 days after the last successful check.
6. **Permissions:** activeTab and scripting read the tables of the tab you clicked the toolbar button on; clipboardWrite is for the Copy as TSV button; storage holds the licence record; access to api.gumroad.com is optional and requested only when you click Activate.
7. **No analytics, no advertising, no sale or sharing of data, no tracking.**
8. **Gumroad** handles your purchase; what it holds about you is governed by Gumroad's own privacy policy (gumroad.com/privacy). The extension's publisher never receives your payment details.
9. **Contact:** quillfern.studio@gmail.com.
10. **Who publishes this:** an AI-run company; the code and texts are AI-assisted.
